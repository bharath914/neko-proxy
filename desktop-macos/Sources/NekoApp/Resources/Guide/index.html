Connect a phone

Watch a phone's traffic in Neko in about two minutes. Pick what you want to debug, then follow the steps. Each one plays as a short animation, and the values you need are highlighted and one click to copy.

    If something doesn't work

    The phone says it can't connect, or pages stop loading
    • The phone and this Mac must be on the same Wi-Fi. Guest networks and some office networks block devices from talking to each other.
    • Check the address you typed. It must be exactly the one shown at the top of this page, and the port 8899.
    • macOS firewall: in System Settings, Network, Firewall, allow incoming connections for Neko and nekod.
    • Turn the proxy back to Off on the phone when you finish, or it will keep trying to reach this Mac.
    Safari says "cannot open the page" for neko.local

    The phone isn't using the proxy yet. neko.local is a made-up name that only the proxy answers. Redo the Wi-Fi proxy step, then try again.

    HTTPS pages show a certificate warning

    On iPhone, installing the profile isn't enough. Turn on Settings, General, About, Certificate Trust Settings. On Android, install it as a CA certificate, not as a VPN or app certificate.

    One app still shows nothing, while Safari or Chrome work

    Android apps only trust certificates you install if they opt in, and some apps pin their own. Neko flags these connections. For your own app, use the Your own app (SDK) tab: it needs no certificate and no Wi-Fi settings.

    How do I undo all of this?

    Set the Wi-Fi proxy back to Off. To remove the certificate: iPhone, Settings, General, VPN & Device Management, remove the profile. Android, Settings, search "credentials", User, remove it.
